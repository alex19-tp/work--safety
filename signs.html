Types of Safety Signs

Safety signs are everywhere: on the road, at the workplace, on buses and other means of transport. Click the buttons below to filter by category!

Warning Signs

Radioactivity
Electrocution
Slipping

Shape & Colour: Triangular shape and a yellow background.

Meaning: They warn us about possible dangers and threats (e.g., Radioactivity, Electrocution, Slipping, Fire and Explosion, Biohazards).

Prohibition Signs

No Smoking
No Admission
No Phones

Shape & Colour: Round shape with a red frame on a white background.

Meaning: They tell us what we must not do or what is forbidden (e.g., No smoking, No dogs allowed, No admission, No mobile phones).

Mandatory Signs

Wear Goggles
Wear Gloves
Turn Right

Shape & Colour: Round shape and a blue background.

Meaning: They tell us what we must do or wear (compulsory PPE), such as Wash hands, Wear safety boots, Wear gloves, Wear goggles, Turn right.

Emergency & Rescue Signs

First Aid Kit
Emergency Exit
Assembly Point

Shape & Colour: Square or rectangular shape and a green background.

Meaning: They tell us the way out of a danger or give first aid info (e.g., Emergency exit, Assembly point, First aid kit, Life jacket).

Fire Safety Signs

Fire Extinguisher

Shape & Colour: Rectangular/square shape with a red background.

Meaning: Designed to catch immediate attention even at a distance (e.g., Fire extinguisher).

Information Signs

Road Directions
Open / Closed

Shape & Colour: May vary in shape, colour, and information.

Meaning: Unlike safety signs, they give general important information we may need (e.g., Road directions, Open/Closed, Out of order).
